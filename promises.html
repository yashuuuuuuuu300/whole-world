My Promises to You 💖

Main tera respect kabhi kam nahi hone dunga.
Main har roz tujhe ye feel karwaunga ki tu mere liye kitni important hai.
Main tujhe kabhi judge nahi karunga, tere har emotion ko samajhne ki koshish karunga.
Main tere sapno mein tere saathi ki tarah hamesha support karunga.
Main tere chehre ki muskan kabhi kam nahi hone dunga.
Main tujhe hamesha celebrate karunga — bina kisi wajah ke bhi.
Main teri har baat sununga, bina interrupt kiye.
Main tere liye roz kuch naya aur special feel karne ki koshish karunga.
Main tujhmein har roz aur zyada pyaar dhoondta rahunga.
Main tujhe kabhi nahi chodunga — chahe kuch bhi ho jaye.

Main tujhe hamesha proud feel karwaunga ki tu meri ho.
Main kabhi tere trust ko todne ki koshish nahi karunga.

Main tujhe har lamha feel karwaunga ki tu hi meri duniya hai.
Main tujhse hamesha sach bolunga — chahe sach mushkil ho.
